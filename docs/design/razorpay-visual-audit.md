# Razorpay public-site visual audit

Observed: 2026-08-27  
Source: [Razorpay public website](https://razorpay.com/)

## Observations used

- Strong royal blue is used selectively for major calls to action and selected/product emphasis.
- Dark blue-green sections establish contrast around dense financial content.
- White and very pale blue-grey surfaces keep the interface operational rather than decorative.
- Heading typography is expressive and compact; product copy uses a neutral interface sans serif.
- Controls favor modest 4–12px corner radii rather than highly rounded consumer-app geometry.
- Major calls to action are approximately 48px tall with clear solid and outlined hierarchy.
- Borders are quiet, shadows are restrained, and content grouping depends primarily on spacing.
- Status and trust cues are concise and located close to the relevant action or result.

## RecoveryOS interpretation

RecoveryOS translates those characteristics into merchant recovery workflows:

- `#305EFF` anchors primary decisions and selected navigation.
- `#032A3E` is used for the app sidebar and high-contrast identity surface.
- `#F8FAFC`, `#F1F5FA`, and `#F0F4F6` separate operational regions without heavy cards.
- The original RecoveryOS mark uses two forward recovery strokes inside a blue tile.
- Verification badges distinguish `SIMULATED`, `RAZORPAY TEST VERIFIED`, and future production evidence.
- Dense case data uses semantic tables, while explanations and rejected alternatives use cards and timelines.

## Explicit exclusions

This is not a Razorpay clone. The implementation excludes Razorpay's logo, wordmark, proprietary font files, illustrations, copied screenshots, source CSS, product-specific navigation, and claims of affiliation. The visible integration badge is factual environment status only.

Before any public launch, the project owner should review the current [Razorpay brand assets terms](https://newsroom.razorpay.in/brand-assets/) and replace or remove any terminology that no longer complies.
